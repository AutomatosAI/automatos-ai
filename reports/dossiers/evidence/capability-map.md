## How to read this

- **28 review units** merged from four enumerator sweeps plus the founder seed list. All 23 founder seeds are present under their original keys (some broadened); 5 discovered units were added (`llm-core`, `evals-learning`, `tool-runtime`, `composio-integration`, `onboarding-intake`). Seed accounting is at the bottom — nothing was dropped, everything trivial is folded into a parent and listed.
- **key** is stable and becomes the dossier filename. **Group** is one of intelligence / execution / ingress-egress / platform / vertical.
- **Tier**: `deep` (15) = load-bearing, differentiating, or known-pain — gets the full dossier treatment; `standard` (13) = full coverage, lighter depth.
- **Status**: `live` = mounted/started and used in production paths; `partial` = merged but dark-launched, half-wired, or missing a leg; `vestigial` = no production caller (none of the 28 units is vestigial as a whole; vestigial sub-parts are named inside units as kill-list candidates).
- **July refs**: section numbers (§) and finding ids (F0xx) in `PLATFORM_OS_REVIEW_2026-07-01.md`. Many findings have since been closed by waves — each unit's blurb says which; re-verify closures against live code, never trust the PRD text.
- **North Star for every dossier**: does this module make Auto and the agents more autonomously capable, and their client-facing output higher quality? Honest negatives ("this is fine") are first-class results.
